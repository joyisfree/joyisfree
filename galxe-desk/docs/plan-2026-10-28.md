## 0. Verdict for Leif

1. **As a money-maker, one Galxe account is not worth it, by every count we could find.** One honest account earns about $0 to a few tens of dollars a month before the $0.08-a-claim fees, and no verifiable log of anyone earning more exists anywhere. Three scouts and three independent reviewers all landed there.
2. **The "automated account that enters everything" is the one part that is certainly a loss, and it defeats your own goal.** It is exactly what Galxe's bot system is built to catch; a flagged account can still do tasks but **cannot claim** (the precise failure you want to avoid); Gigapot and Earndrop bans are permanent and eligibility decisions are "final and non-appealable"; and the public "Galxe bots" on GitHub sit in a category proven to steal wallet keys. We do not build it.
3. **"Worthless rewards" are not bad luck, they are the default.** OATs, per-brand points (non-transferable), Galxe Gold (no cash value) and raffles shared by about 1,400 wallets. Real money in 2025 to 2026 went to on-chain usage with capital, NFT holders and hand-picked communities, and three pay-for-posting rails closed this year (X's InfoFi ban 15 Jan, Intract 12 Jan, Galxe Starboard 3 Aug).
4. **What is worth building is the Galxe Desk you described, minus the bot:** the full team plus hired specialists mirroring Galxe's real functions (rules counsel, prize analyst, quest scout, security guardian, a standing critic panel), the whole official rulebook read and kept, and a **Prize Gate** that refuses every quest whose prize is not real, claimable and eligible for your account before a minute is spent. It makes the small game precise, and it costs almost nothing to run.
5. **The run:** 90 days, by hand, 10 minutes a day on only the shortlisted quests, in its own Brave profile with a throwaway wallet of at most $25, a ledger of minutes, fees and realised dollars, and kill rules. Then you decide on your own numbers, not on hype.
6. **The X account:** keep it separate from the studio's handle, always. A brand-new one fails the 1-to-12-month age gates most paying quests use; an aged personal account passes today, a new one passes in 3 to 6 months if it posts like a person. Never automated engagement.
7. **The win-win edge:** the same desk pointed at your agency is a "pay creators only for verified results" engine, which is where the growth reviewer put the real upside ($600 cap, 60 days).
8. Nothing here touches the studio app or its repo.
9. **Three things are yours** (section 8): approve the path; the X account and the wallet (logins and the seed phrase stay with you); one optional cloud setting so cloud sessions can read galxe.com. Everything else is ours.

### 3A. Galxe's own rules and mechanics (Scout A, 28 Oct 2026)

**Rules**
- Terms of Use effective 10 Sep 2025 (RECHECK). "Prohibited Uses" is a representative list Galxe can change at will; the nearest clause to scripting is "Abusive Activity": anything that imposes "an unreasonable or disproportionately large load" or interferes with systems or data. Galxe may "limit the number of User Accounts or Digital Wallets you may link", and may suspend or terminate on "reasonable suspicion" of a prohibited use, some decisions on "confidential criteria". [ToS](https://docs.galxe.com/about/legal/terms-of-service)
- **Bot Identification System** (help/11827806): a bot is "any automated program designed to interact with Galxe quests without real human involvement". Detection is behavioural (timing and sequence across accounts) plus on-chain (rapid address creation, linked "unrelated" addresses). It also flags unusual activity "even when done manually by individuals". Flagged accounts go to a security team; disqualification applies to the GG Rewards Hub, Gigapot and Business+ quests with sybil prevention on; repeat or severe cases get temporary or permanent restrictions.
- **Account restrictions** (help/11925374): most come from "patterns that indicate the use of multiple accounts", plus irregular IPs, device fingerprints and geographic inconsistencies. Restrictions attach to behaviour, not wallets. One appeal, through live chat, with new evidence.
- **One Passport per identity** ($5, Sumsub, $1 per facial re-check, 30-day wait after revoking; help/9424571). Each social account binds to one Galxe account; the primary wallet cannot be changed (help/9733866). Not found verbatim: a "one account per person" sentence or a general clawback clause (the help centre calls multi-accounting a ToS violation; Gigapot rules allow prize withholding).

**What a quester can earn**
- **Space Loyalty Points are per brand**, on-chain (Gravity, Solana, Sui, Sei), claimed with gas, "not transferable"; no conversion to a token found (help/9733873). They are only worth what that one brand later decides.
- **Galxe Gold (GG) and XP** are the platform currencies. GG "has no cash value and can't be transferred"; it is earned only on quests that carry the $0.08 claim fee (+30 XP, +30 GG per qualified claim), spent on **Gigapot** (weekly lottery, 50 GG a ticket) and **Mystery Boxes** (USDT/ETH/G, odds set by the brand). XP sets a Loyalty Level and expires in 30 to 90 days (help/13545714, help/12829115, help/10373007).
- **Referral**: 80 GG after an invitee completes 10 quests; no XP (help/10373007). Self-referral is multi-accounting.
- **Galxe Score**: $5 a year, five dimensions including Humanity (L2 needs face verification), refreshed weekly, and "can drop if the bot system flags you" (help/9699532).
- **Quest reward types**: points, OAT ($0.08 claim fee), NFT, token (on-chain raffle in G, or off-chain first-come-first-served), luck-based split (each winner at least $1), Mystery Box, off-chain custom (whitelist, beta, merch). Recurring quests pay loyalty points only. No official typical-reward figure exists; the 2025 review's 2,700+ raffles over 3.8M wallets is about 1,400 wallets per raffle (help/9645630, help/11872447, [2025 review](https://www.galxe.com/blog/galxe-2025-year-in-review)).
- **Earndrop** (Jan 2025): token-claim hub filtered by Passport and Score; a claim fee may apply (help/10899990).

**State of the platform (why "now" matters)**
- Gravity L1 mainnet 21 Jul 2026; **Gravity Alpha shuts 1 Nov 2026**; quest reward contracts moved to L1 and claims fail if G sits on Alpha (help/16010489, help/16305612). G staking pays no yield (a GG boost only) and its dashboard is closing (26 Oct or 1 Nov, sources conflict; help/9202640). Smart Savings became Smart Balance with no yield (help/8388133). **Starboard, Galxe's InfoFi leaderboard, closed 3 Aug 2026** (help/11426659). Alva and Compass (2024 AI tools) have no verified 2026 status.

**API and feeds**
- The official GraphQL API is **Space-scoped**: a token generated by a Space owner/admin, 10 QPS and 100k requests a month on the free tier; no documented anonymous endpoint lists quests across all Spaces, and no official RSS, email, Telegram or push feed for new quests exists ([auth](https://docs.galxe.com/galxe-integration/getting-started/authentication), [rate limits](https://docs.galxe.com/galxe-integration/resources/rate-limits)). Read-only polling by a user's own script is not addressed either way; the "Abusive Activity" clause is judged at Galxe's discretion.

**For brands**
- A Space is permissionless: an EVM wallet, project details, a bound official email (help/8506982). Pricing: Basic $0 a month with a 300 USDT/USDC deposit; Essentials about $999 to $1,166 a month; Growth $1,699 to $1,999 a month; Verified Space 350 USDC a year, optional (help/11568587, help/10673534).
- Per-quest participation gates a brand can set: **X account age (1, 3, 6 or 12 months), followers (20, 50, 100 or 500), blue check**, Humanity Score L1 to L3, Galxe+ tier, Passport, Gitcoin/Human Passport, TrustScan, Nomis; Business+ Advanced Sybil Prevention lets flagged accounts do tasks but not claim (help/9729011, help/11586930). With no gate set, "multiple or new accounts can participate freely".
- Case studies are self-reported and 2022 to 2023 (Optimism 3.4M mints, Odos 900 to 163K X followers, Linea Voyage 5.6M wallets and 800K sybil-credential claims); no retention metric is published (help/collections/6667947).

**The team to mirror** (third-party sources, RECHECK): co-founders Charles Wayn and Harry Zhang (CEO), Xinlu C. (tech); Yumin Xia CTO; Yifei Zhou Head of Product; Andrew Kim Head of Ecosystem; Jared Schwartz Head of Knowledge and Support; a security team that reviews flagged accounts; past postings for regional community managers, client experience, partner success, data science. **No open roles anywhere on 28 Oct 2026.** Functions: community, ecosystem/BD and partner success, product, data, security/anti-bot, support, engineering; nothing titled growth, quest design, research or compliance.

**What this means for one person with one account (Scout A's own summary, kept):** one real person using one account by hand is the only route the rules clearly allow; multi-account patterns are the stated top reason for restrictions and follow behaviour, not wallets; Galxe defines a bot as an automated program and also flags human activity that looks scripted; single-user payouts are small and lottery-shaped (GG has no cash value, claims cost $0.08, Gigapot and Mystery Boxes are random); the money, if any, sits in individual brand campaigns and Earndrops, which increasingly require Score, Passport or an aged X account; the infrastructure is mid-migration; the API serves brands, not questers, and no alert feed exists.

### 3B. Community numbers and risk (Scout B, 28 Oct 2026)

Method note: same limits as 3A (search digests only; reddit.com unreachable; the session's shared web-search budget ran out before the "what full-time farmers say" question was finished). Numbers marked "my arithmetic" are the scout's division, not a published figure.

**What one account actually earns**
- **No verifiable per-account earnings report for Galxe-only questing exists**, and no hours-per-week figure. Scale is huge (about 184M quest participations and 1.9M unique addresses a month in 2025, [Messari](https://messari.io/report/galxe-gravity-general-update), RECHECK), which is the point: rewards are split across millions.
- **The platform's own currency is worth cents.** G trades at about $0.004 (CoinGecko, Bybit, Oct 2026), so a 60 G Mystery Box prize is about $0.25; a 99 GG box costs about 3.3 claims, about $0.26 in L1 claim fees. Break-even at best, before gas and time (box table from a 2025 opinion post, RECHECK). Gigapot pool sizes are not published.
- **The pools that paid in 2025 to 2026 were set by the project, not Galxe**: Sahara AI's Earndrop (500M SAHARA to 1.4M+ addresses, based on Sahara's own activity to 31 May 2025, 44% at TGE and the rest vested), Tanssi (about $1.7M), XRP Earndrop (400,000 XRP pool). The one 2026 pool found: AIW3, "60,000 USDT" in AIW3 tokens, about 3,640 participants, claimable after TGE, at most about $16.5 each if split evenly (my arithmetic; split rule unstated) ([quest page](https://app.galxe.com/quest/ck3c8enjXPDnduV3AiQoie/GCPnQtZ2uL)). Old yardstick: an Aptos quest of $4,000 over 400 winners, $10 each.
- Sentiment: Trustpilot 3.2/5 from 15 reviews (paid KYC, bans); Product Hunt reviews cite bot-heavy participation.

**Bans and sybil hunts**
- Galxe's Bot Identification System flags manual bot-like behaviour too; flagged users can do tasks but see "Bot activity detected… requires human participation" at claim. **Gigapot restrictions cannot be lifted; Earndrop bans are irreversible; eligibility decisions, including algorithmic scoring, are "final and non-appealable"** (help/11827806, help/11586930, help/10899990, ToS).
- The projects did the real filtering: LayerZero flagged 803,093 sybil addresses (2024); Linea cut 516,960 of 1,297,203 at first pass and needed Proof-of-Humanity plus LXP thresholds (Sep 2025); Starknet used Trusta lists and a balance rule; Scroll scored on-chain bridging, liquidity and lending. **No airdrop found that paid for social quests alone**; points, on-chain activity and proof-of-humanity decided.
- Whether a lone automated account gets caught is not quantified anywhere; Galxe says it flags even manual behaviour.

**The "bots" on GitHub**: dante4rt/Galxe-AutoComplete-Tasks (about 223 stars), the-laziest-coder/galxe-aio (about 301 stars) and smaller clones claim to auto-complete and auto-claim. No evidence they were banned, and none was inspected. The category is poisoned: SlowMist's July 2025 analysis of a GitHub "trading bot" that uploaded private keys via an obfuscated npm dependency with faked stars; compromised @solana/web3.js releases (Dec 2024) that stole keys from bots; repos posing as "airdrop claim tools" with infostealer behaviour; one with an `EXFIL_ENDPOINT` setting. Never run one.

**X / Twitter in 2026**
- [X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation) (updated about Sep 2026): allowed, automated informational posts and one opt-in reply per interaction; **not allowed, bulk or aggressive following, duplicate posts across your accounts, bulk/keyword/scheduled auto-likes**. The [authenticity policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity) bans coordinated or compensated exchange of likes, reposts and follows and multi-account amplification; severe cases are permanent at first detection. Follow cap 400 a day.
- **On 15 Jan 2026 X revoked API access for apps that reward posting** (Kaito, Cookie; KAITO fell 15 to 20%) ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/385805/kaito-token-plummets-x-revises-api-policies-ban-infofi-crypto-projects), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/354736/elon-musks-x-bans-access-infofi-crypto-projects-ai-slop-backlash)). X's developer policy also bars "pay to engage". Effect on Galxe's X verification: not found.
- X API is pay-per-use with no free tier for new developers (reads $0.005 a post, posts $0.015); irrelevant to a human using the app, relevant to anyone thinking of "an automated account".
- **Galxe quests can require an X account 1, 3, 6 or 12 months old with 20 to 500 followers** (help/9729011). A brand-new dedicated account fails every quest with an age gate for months. No reports found of new accounts suspended specifically for quest bursts; one forum case of a new account suspended after a few posts and a follow, appeal took months.

**Security**: the Oct 2023 Galxe DNS hijack (registrar social-engineered; $396K from about 980 users, refunded with 10%); lookalike claim pages (galxe-claim.app, airdrop-galxe.one) still drain wallets; 2025 drainer losses $83.85M across 106,106 victims, average about $790, largest $6.5M from one Permit signature ([ScamSniffer](https://drops.scamsniffer.io/scam-sniffer-2025-crypto-phishing-losses-fall-83-to-84-million/)). Hygiene Galxe itself publishes: bookmark app.galxe.com, verify handles, refuse approval pop-ups on navigation, fresh low-balance wallet, treat every signature as a possible approval, revoke after anything odd, staff never DM. Passport sends a government ID to Sumsub.

**Opportunity cost**: no credible single-account $/hour estimate exists; the multi-wallet narratives come from proxy and anti-detect vendors. The hard numbers point one way: Linea cut 42%, LayerZero flagged 803K, zkSync excluded about 90%, and X ended reward-for-posting in Jan 2026.

**Scout B's bottom line (kept):** as income, Galxe is a no-go for one person; Galxe's own currency is nearly worthless; the payouts that mattered were set by each project's own activity rules; honest manual single-account use carries low ban risk but a flag is costly and often final; do not automate; a brand-new X account fits poorly; the real tail risk is a drained wallet; skip Passport/KYC unless one campaign justifies it; Galxe's token side is shrinking; if he still wants to try, cap it at 1 to 2 hours a week for a month, log time and dollars, then decide on real numbers.

### 3C. Where the money actually went, both sides of the platform (Scout C, 28 Oct 2026)

Method note: same limits (search digests; the session's search budget ran out before X creator revenue share, Binance Square and fashion brands beyond Nike were covered; marked "not searched"). † = older than 6 months or undated, RECHECK.

**How the best research desks rank a quest or points programme**
- Published criteria: funding and VC quality ($50M+ raised as a "top tier" screen in vendor guides), a working product and TVL, a confirmed token versus "potential", points-to-token conversion history, capital required, cost and minutes per activity (CryptoRank Drophunting lists $10 to $15 and 15 to 30 minutes per activity), sybil risk, and the team's past behaviour. Airdrops.com scores Potential, Credibility and Simplicity 1 to 5. DefiLlama's airdrops page filters tokenless protocols by TVL and money raised and gives vetted claim links. Bankless Airdrop Hunter asks two things: a non-trivial chance of a token within 6 to 12 months, and how much capital to commit, never more than you can lose. The expected-value rule in the guides: P(eligible) × allocation × token value − costs − time − risk. Points have no price ("more art than science", Blockworks†).
- Delphi, 3 Jun 2026: 78 to 94% of recipient wallets in the largest airdrops sold most of their allocation by day 90 (3.7M wallets). Count only what is realised.

**What the well-paid did (with the median, not the headline)**
- **Hyperliquid** (Nov 2024†): 94,019 addresses; mean 2,915 HYPE (about $20,000), **median 64.5 HYPE (about $400)**; points came from perp trading with real capital; no social tasks.
- **Berachain** (Feb 2025†): NFT holders 6.9% of supply; testnet users 1.65% across 2.38M wallets (about 3.46 BERA, under $1 to about $54 each); **social commentary 0.25%**.
- **Monad** (claims Oct to Nov 2025†, official): hand-picked Community track 5,935 accounts at about $7,000 each; **on-chain users 229,433 accounts at about $186 each**, 41% claimed; 52.4% of claimers sold or moved everything.
- **Linea** (Sep 2025†): 749,662 addresses after Nansen flagged 516,960 of 1,297,203 (about 40%); needed 2,000+ LXP or 15,000+ LXP-L plus Proof-of-Humanity; recipients were roughly 13% of the 5.6M Voyage wallets Galxe reports.
- **Kaito**: from Apr 2025, $5,000 a week split across the top 100 yappers, about $50 each; the X-based programme ended 15 Jan 2026.
- 2026 designs pay usage and lock supply: Jupiter cut Jupuary to fee-paying activity; MegaETH excluded coordinated, duplicate or automated wallets and locked 53% of supply behind targets.
- Social-task-only quests: no published median payout anywhere; Galxe luck-based rewards pay at least $1 per winner; one scraper example shows 6,700 wallets chasing 10 slots, about a 0.15% chance.

**Brand side**
- Galxe Business+: Essentials $999 a month billed annually ($11,988 a year), Growth $1,699 a month annually ($20,388 a year); Basic free with a 300 USDT deposit; fees non-refundable; rewards and gas extra; users pay the claim fee. Zealy: Standard $149 a month, Plus $359 a month, free tier without X tasks. Layer3: 120M+ completions, $285M in rewards, 98.6% of reward value from client budgets. Kaito Studio: brands post budgeted campaigns and pay approved creators for results.
- Outcomes: Galxe's Optimism case (3.4M mints, daily wallets 11.65k to 25.13k) is vendor-published and an independent view says activity fell back when rewards ended. An arXiv study of one quest system (80M completions) found participation fell to 5% of peak and a $500-a-day pool emptied faster each day. A vendor benchmark: open quests retain under 5% at day 30, gated ones 10 to 20%; sign-ups cost $0.05 to $0.50, funded wallets $8 to $40. **Every Galxe case found is crypto-native; no non-crypto consumer-brand case exists.**
- Fashion and phygital: Nike wound down RTFKT (Dec 2024), retired .SWOOSH, was sued, then sold RTFKT (Dec 2025). Other fashion programmes: not searched.

**Alternatives for one person, one account**: Galxe (lottery with fees; Galxe+ $1.69 to $79.99 a month; Score $5 a year); Alva (no payout data); Kaito/InfoFi (dead on X since 15 Jan 2026; Studio is selective); Layer3 CUBEs (2024 airdrop about $1.25 a user, crude; nothing since); Zealy (payouts set per community, no statistics); **Intract Quest closed 12 Jan 2026**; testnets (under $1 to $186 a wallet). X creator revenue share and Binance Square: not searched. Net: no 2025 to 2026 source shows ordinary single-account social-quest users earning meaningful sums.

**Trend**: X (Nikita Bier, 15 Jan 2026): "We will no longer allow apps that reward users for posting on X (aka 'infofi')"; Kaito ended Yaps, Cookie shut Snaps, Intract closed, Galxe Starboard inactive since 3 Aug 2026. Galxe's own direction is gated and gamified (Passport V3, bot identification, XP, claim fees, Gigapot), not retiring social tasks. Zealy is hardening (holding periods, AI-reviewed quote tweets). No traffic series exists; only self-reported figures (36M users, 1M daily active).

**Operating model the evidence supports**: feeds (DefiLlama airdrops, CryptoRank Drophunting, Bankless monthly hunts, Airdrop Alert, Galxe Explore); one person, one device, no scripts (restrictions follow behaviour; IP, device and geography are signals); an assistant reads and ranks, a human clicks; clocks (raffle claims last 15 days, Gigapot tickets 7 days); a weekly ranking sheet (platform, pool, winners, participants, fill ratio, claim fee, deadline, minutes, gas, realised dollars), a daily 10-minute pass, a 90-day kill rule. No published daily routine was found; this is the scout's inference.

**Scout C's "what the top 10% would tell this founder" (kept):** real money followed capital and real usage; read the median, not the headline; most recipients sell, count only what is realised; social-task-only is the bottom tier and the pay-for-posting rails are going away; one Galxe account is a small lottery with fees, cap it at 10 minutes a day or skip it; never run multiple accounts; on the brand side Galxe starts at $11,988 a year plus rewards and gas with under 5% day-30 retention and no garment-buyer case anywhere; the best-documented consumer-brand result is Nike's retreat; own your list and your app accounts; if you want upside, pay creators for verified results and keep any airdrop time capped with a 90-day kill rule.

### 3D. Verified against the official docs (Context7 index of docs.galxe.com, 28 Oct 2026)

Context7 holds an indexed copy of Galxe's documentation (`/websites/galxe`, 1,168 snippets, high reputation), which gets round this session's network block for a first pass. Confirmed from the official text:
- **Terms of Service, prohibited uses** ([terms](https://docs.galxe.com/about/legal/terms-of-service)): "using unauthorized third-party applications"; using the services "under a different identifier if access was disabled"; leasing access to a wallet; sharing passwords or private keys; "Abusive Activity" (unreasonable load, interfering with systems, unauthorised access, using third-party accounts, transferring access). Galxe may suspend, restrict or cancel accounts for suspected prohibited uses, "circumvention of Galxe's controls" and "abuse of promotions". - **Quest types in the API**: Drop (NFT/standard), MysteryBox, Airdrop (token), Points, ExternalLink, Bounty. Quest fields: `status` (NotStarted, Active, Expired, **CapReached**, Draft, Deleted), `startTime`, `endTime`, `cap`, `participantsCount`, `loyaltyPoints`, `gasType`, `chain`, `space`.
- **The official eligibility query** ([guide](https://docs.galxe.com/galxe-integration/guides/quests)): `CheckUserEligibility(questId, address)` returns, per credential group, each condition's `eligible` flag and each reward's `eligible` flag, with `conditionRelation` ALL or ANY. "When `rewards.eligible` is true, user can claim rewards." **This is the documented way to know, before and after doing the tasks, whether our address can claim**, and it is the backbone of the Prize Gate (step 4 below; access-token question is a RECHECK).
- Rewards Hub Mystery Boxes are sweepstakes in law: the terms provide a postcard "Alternate Method of Entry", one per entrant per year.
