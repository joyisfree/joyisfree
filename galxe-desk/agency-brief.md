# The desk's second client: the agency, creators paid only for verified results

From the growth reviewer's cross-check of 28 Oct 2026, kept as a brief; its own plan when the desk runs.

- **The model:** 12 micro-creators over 60 days, each with a unique code; paid only on tracked orders or app sign-ups
  (say 15% of order value, or $5 a sign-up); never for likes, reposts or follows (X bars paid engagement). Cap $600.
- **The checker:** an AI results-checker maps codes to orders and sign-ups, screens fake followers, and writes a weekly
  cost-per-verified-result report. Same discipline as the Prize Gate: a result is counted only when it is real.
- **The rule at day 60:** keep creators costing under 30% of average order value per sale; move their buyers onto
  Joyisfree's own list and app accounts (own the relationship, not the platform).
- **Then the agency:** sell the home-and-garden clients the same pay-on-verified-result terms once the numbers hold.
- **Why it is the win-win edge:** the quester side teaches the funnel from the user's seat; the brand side is where the
  same judgement earns, with no platform between Joyisfree and its people.
