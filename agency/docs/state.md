# State — 5 Nov 2026 (the agency track)
- **Why this track exists:** on 31 Oct the Galxe questing lane was refused on the numbers (`../galxe-desk/docs/decision-2026-10-31-questing.md`). The brand side is where the leverage is.
- **Finding that set the plan (5 Nov):** the cheapest decisive test named on 31 Oct — does a limited drop beat a plain email to the same list? — cannot be settled at Joyisfree's scale. A clothing campaign email converts about 0.12% of recipients (Klaviyo 2026, search summary; range 0.07–0.13%), so detecting a doubling of orders needs about 19,600 per arm (~39,000 on the list). The first draft of the analyser hid this: its sample-size formula understated the need by up to a third, and its example baseline was 30 times too high. A 44-agent read-only review confirmed 15 of 18 defects in it; 3 were refuted and not built.
- **Built:** `scripts/ab-test.mjs` (pre-registered, one look, strict dates, refuses before the send; normal answer TOO SMALL, plain email stands) and `scripts/creator-pilot.mjs` (pays only on verified orders and sign-ups, whole-cent money, $600 cap checked before any payout, refund clawback, day-30 and day-60 rules, reach never paid).
- **Next:** Leif supplies the store's pre-pilot average order value; then `pilot/pilot.json` is fixed and dated before the first creator is added. The drop-vs-email test runs only if `--preflight` says the list is big enough.
- **Traps:** never blend flows into a campaign baseline; never look before `analyseOn`; never attach a prize draw to a drop (state permit rules conflict); `pilot/` is gitignored and must stay so.
