# Saturday market stall

1. Load the van Friday night: two racks, the table, the mirror and the float.
2. Arrive by 6:30 am; the stall is number 14, next to the coffee cart.
3. Hang the hoodies by size, XS to XXL, black first, then bone.
4. Put the card reader on charge the night before; the spare is in the blue box.
5. Price tags on every piece. The float is $200: $100 in notes, $100 in coins.
6. Take three photos of the stall for Instagram before 8 am.
7. Pack down at 2 pm, count the stock against the list, and text Claude the numbers.
8. Claude updates the stock sheet and exports the sales to the shared folder.
