# Qalam sources

Researched 6 to 7 Nov 2026. Every ayah and hadith below was matched by web search against sunnah.com or quran.com, or
against a mirror that uses sunnah.com's numbering, before the skill used it. A line without "verified" must not be
quoted. Studies carry their caveat on the same line. Re-check anything here after May 2027.

## Qur'an (Sahih International)

- **Qur'an 68:1**, verified: "Nun. By the pen and what they inscribe." https://legacy.quran.com/68/1
- **Qur'an 96:4** (with 96:3 to 96:5), verified: "Recite, and your Lord is the most Generous, Who taught by the pen,
  taught man that which he knew not." https://legacy.quran.com/96/3-5

## Hadith and athar

- **Bukhari 1142**, verified: Satan ties three knots on the sleeper. Remembering Allah unties one, wudu the second,
  prayer the third, "and one gets up energetic with a good heart." https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1142
- **Bukhari 6314**, verified (the waking dua): the Prophet put his hand under his cheek at bedtime and said "Bismika
  amutu wa ahya". On waking he said "Al-hamdu lillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushur". The same words
  are in Bukhari 6324. https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6314
- **Bukhari 247**, verified (mirror): before bed, wudu as for prayer, lie on the right side and say the supplication
  taught to Al-Bara'. Also Bukhari 6311. https://www.islamicfinder.org/hadith/bukhari/wudu-ablution/247
- **Bukhari 568**, verified: the Prophet disliked sleeping before Isha and talking after it. Commentators link the
  dislike to missing Isha or, through late talk, Fajr. https://sunnah.com/bukhari:568
- **Bukhari 6464**, verified: "the most beloved deed to Allah is the most regular and constant even if it were
  little." https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6464
- **Abu Dawud 2606**, verified (graded sahih by al-Albani): "O Allah, bless my people in their early mornings." The
  narrator, Sakhr al-Ghamidi, a merchant, sent his goods out early in the day and became wealthy. Also in Tirmidhi 1212
  (hasan). https://sunnah.com/abudawud:2606
- **Abu Dawud 4700**, verified (graded sahih by al-Albani): "The first thing Allah created was the pen. He said to it:
  Write." https://sunnah.com/abudawud:4700
- **Bukhari 941**, verified: "We used to offer the Jumu'a prayer with the Prophet and then take the afternoon nap"
  (the qailulah). https://sunnah.com/bukhari:941
- **Umar ibn al-Khattab**, verified (athar, mirror): "Hold yourselves to account before you are held to account."
  At-Tirmidhi cites it after Tirmidhi 2459; it is also in Ahmad's Kitab al-Zuhd.
  https://hadithanswers.com/take-account-of-yourself-before-your-accountability-is-taken-by-allah/

## Studies

- **Scullin 2018:** Scullin, Krueger, Ballard, Pruett and Bliwise, J Exp Psychol Gen 147(1):139-146. Writing a
  specific to-do list at bedtime led to sleep about 9 minutes faster than writing about completed tasks. *Caveat:*
  57 healthy young adults, one night in a lab, not tested in insomnia.
  https://news.web.baylor.edu/news/story/2018/can-writing-your-dos-help-you-doze-baylor-study-suggests-jotting-down-tasks-can
- **Windred 2024:** Windred et al., Sleep 47(1):zsad253. Sleep regularity predicted all-cause mortality better than
  sleep duration. *Caveat:* about 61,000 UK Biobank adults, observational; irregular sleep may be a marker of ill
  health rather than its cause.
  https://researchnow.flinders.edu.au/en/publications/sleep-regularity-is-a-stronger-predictor-of-mortality-risk-than-s/
- **Gollwitzer 2006:** Gollwitzer and Sheeran, Adv Exp Soc Psychol 38:69-119. If-then plans ("after X, I do Y")
  showed a medium-to-large effect on reaching goals (d = 0.65 across 94 tests). *Caveat:* the effect varies by goal
  and person.
- **Lally 2010:** Lally, van Jaarsveld, Potts and Wardle, Eur J Soc Psychol 40(6):998-1009. Habits took a median of
  66 days to become automatic (range 18 to 254), and missing one opportunity did not materially affect the process.
  *Caveat:* 96 volunteers, simple behaviours, self-report.
- **Wright 2013:** Wright et al., Curr Biol 23(16):1554-1558. A week of natural light only (camping) moved body clocks
  earlier, in step with the sun. *Caveat:* 8 people.
- **Watson 2015:** the joint AASM and Sleep Research Society consensus, Sleep 38(6):843-844. Adults need 7 hours or
  more of sleep, regularly.
- **Qaseem 2016:** American College of Physicians guideline, Ann Intern Med 165(2):125-133. CBT-I is the first-line
  treatment for chronic insomnia in adults.
- **Brooks 2006:** Brooks and Lack, Sleep 29(6):831-840. After a short night, a 10-minute afternoon nap gave the best
  overall benefit. *Caveat:* small lab study.

## Methods (books, not studies)

- BJ Fogg, *Tiny Habits* (2019): an anchor, a tiny behaviour, and a celebration straight after it.
- James Clear, *Atomic Habits* (2018): habit stacking, making the cue obvious (the notebook where they pray),
  never miss twice.
- David Allen, *Getting Things Done* (2001, revised 2015): every task written as its next physical action.
- Ryder Carroll, *The Bullet Journal Method* (2018): migration. Rewriting by hand forces a decision on each task.

## Not claimed, on purpose

- **Handwriting vs typing.** The evidence is about brain activity only (small EEG studies in 2024, and a 2025 review
  in *Life*). It does not show that handwritten lists get done more often. Qalam's reasons for paper are the pen's
  place in the faith and keeping the phone out of the first minutes of the day.
- **Prayer times.** None are stored here. Any sum of sleep hours uses the user's own masjid timetable.
